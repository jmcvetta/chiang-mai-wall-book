# Chronology of construction and interventions

Status: unreviewed research staging, started 2026-10-08 for issue #3. Not book content.

The inventory reconnaissance did not establish any construction date. This file therefore holds **attributed statements**, not established events. Each row says who claims what, about which component, and how far the claim reaches. None of them dates the fabric standing now.

Rules for this file:

- Keep the three questions apart: when a structure first stood on the site, when the material visible now was put in place, and when that material was repaired.
- Give the geographical extent the source actually names. A statement about "the gates" is not a statement about every wall run.
- Keep the source's own calendar. No calendar conversion is recorded here; conversions need checking before use.
- Repetition is not corroboration. Rows that share a lineage are marked.

Source keys refer to `research/shared/sources/register.md`.
